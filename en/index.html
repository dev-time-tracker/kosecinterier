How we work

From the first measurement to fitted furniture.

A clear process means fewer surprises. At every step you know what comes next.

  1. 01

    Call and consultation

    Call or email us and briefly describe what you have in mind. We arrange a visit to see and measure the room.

  2. 02

    Measuring and proposal

    We measure the room precisely, choose materials and fittings with you and prepare a proposal with a quote.

  3. 03

    Making

    We make the furniture to the agreed plan and measurements, in the materials you have chosen.

  4. 04

    Fitting

    We deliver, install and adjust the furniture. At handover we go over every detail together.

Materials

We choose the material for the job.

For every piece we suggest a material that suits its use, look and budget, and we tell you plainly what we fit and why.

Solid wood
For tables, beds, worktops and visible parts, where the warmth of real wood matters.
Chipboard
A stable base for wardrobe and kitchen carcasses, in many finishes and colours.
MDF and laminate
Smooth lacquered fronts and hard-wearing surfaces for kitchens and bathrooms.
Veneer
The natural look of wood on large surfaces where solid wood is not practical.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where do you work?

We work in Ljubljana and the surrounding area and across central Slovenia. If your home is further away, call us and we will check whether we can take on your project.

What materials do you use?

We work mostly with solid wood and chipboard, and depending on the use also with MDF, veneer and laminate. We choose the material together, based on use, look and budget.

Do you make single pieces of furniture?

Yes. We make a single piece, such as a table, a wardrobe or shelving, or fit out an entire flat. We are not limited to a catalogue.

Do you assemble furniture you have not made?

Yes. We also assemble and install shop-bought furniture, and by arrangement we can remove old furniture and handle transport.

How much does a custom kitchen or wardrobe cost?

The price depends on size, materials, fittings and how complex the work is. After measuring and choosing materials we prepare a quote, so you know exactly what to expect.

How do we start?

Call 031 882 452 or email info@kosecinterier.si. If you have a floor plan, measurements or photos of the room, send them along, as they help with the first proposal.
